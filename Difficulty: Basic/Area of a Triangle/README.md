<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/area-of-a-triangle5739/1">Area of a Triangle</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Basic</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Given three sides of a triangle <strong>a</strong>, <strong>b</strong> and <strong>c</strong> in the form of integers. Find the area of the triangle. The triangle may not exist. In such cases, the area is 0.<br><strong>Note:</strong> Output the area up to 3 decimal places without rounding off.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input:</strong> a = 2, b = 2, c = 3
<strong>Output:</strong> 1.984
<strong>Explanation: </strong>The triangle with sides 2, 2, and 3 has an area of 1.984313... square units. Since we don't round off, this becomes 1.984.</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input:</strong> a = 1, b = 3, c = 1
<strong>Output:</strong> 0.000
<strong>Explanation:</strong> No triangle can be formed with sides 1, 3, and 1 (since 1 + 1 &lt; 3), so the area is 0.</span></pre>
<p><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Constraints:<br></strong>1 ≤ a, b, c ≤ 10<sup>4</sup></span></p></div><br><p><span style=font-size:18px><strong>Topic Tags : </strong><br><code>Mathematics</code>&nbsp;
